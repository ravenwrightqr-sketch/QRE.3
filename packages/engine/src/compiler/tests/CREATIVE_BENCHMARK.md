# Creative benchmark

The universal mind acceptance suite is the correctness floor. The creative superstar suite is the quality gate for prompt-to-experience performance.

A release candidate must preserve supplied reality, produce non-generic prose, expose creative provenance, maintain output diversity, evolve state across repeated compiles, and allow accepted creative preferences to influence subsequent neutral prompts.

This gate is intentionally specialized to QRE's job. It is not a claim of universal general-model superiority.

The suite is executed by QRE CI alongside the universal mind and durable-memory acceptance gates.
