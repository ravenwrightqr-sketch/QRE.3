# QRE Author Laws

## Core law

**QRE may surprise us.**

Guardrails protect source truth; they are not a stylistic cage. The Author is allowed to take a creative bet, compress aggressively, use fragments, break ordinary sentence structure, omit articles, choose an unexpected metaphor, or land an unusually strong payoff when the result is grounded enough to defend and materially improves the viewer experience.

A line that is hot, on fire, or fabulous is allowed to win. No stylistic preference is absolute.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

1. **Source truth is immutable.** Do not fabricate consequential reality.
2. **Every rendered cut must retain provenance.** A creative line must still be defensible from supplied reality.
3. **Planner language never reaches the viewer.** Internal cognition, planning, scoring, and authoring terminology stay internal.
4. **Novelty is allowed.** Grounded interpretation may exceed literal wording when the supplied reality provides a real evidence bridge.
5. **New concrete claims require evidence.** New actions, objects, people, locations, chronology, reactions, states, or outcomes cannot be smuggled in as style.
6. **Taste is a score, not a law.** Shortness, fragments, tempo, humor, metaphor, hyperbole, and other stylistic preferences are optimization signals, not universal prohibitions.

## Creative classification

QRE should distinguish:

- **FACT** — directly supplied reality.
- **DERIVED** — meaningfully inferable from multiple supplied facts.
- **CREATIVE** — viewer-facing framing, compression, attitude, metaphor, or interpretation that remains defensible from supplied reality.
- **NEW CLAIM** — a concrete world assertion not supported by supplied reality.

The system should aggressively reward strong FACT + DERIVED + CREATIVE realizations while strongly penalizing unsupported NEW CLAIMS.

## Editing rule

**Every source file touched in the canonical Author path must carry a header stating its role and, where relevant, this law: QRE may surprise us.**

When an existing file is touched, preserve its actual responsibility or rewrite it to the canonical responsibility; do not add compatibility layers merely to avoid making the architecture coherent.
