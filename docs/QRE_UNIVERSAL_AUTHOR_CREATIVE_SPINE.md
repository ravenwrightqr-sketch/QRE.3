# QRE Universal Author Creative Spine

STATUS: CANONICAL
DATE: 2026-09-05
BRANCH: `build/universal-author-local`

QRE is not a domain-specific story generator. It turns supplied reality into media that the subject, owner, or participant can want to experience again.

## Product boundary

The frontend stays deliberately simple:

```text
QRE
What are we making?
[ one obvious input ]
```

The user supplies ordinary language. QRE may ask one useful missing question. The user never needs to understand Movie, Frame, RealityGraph, Cognition, Lens, or Mouth.

Website knowledge and other learned world context are quiet context. They describe the arena; they do not become a second creative authority.

## Universal food chain

```text
FRONTEND INTAKE
    |
    v

    |
    v
SOURCE REALITY
(facts + moments + memory + learned world + place/time/media)
    |
    v
REALITY GRAPH
(explicit evidence + conservative derived relations)
    |
    v
METAMORPHIC RELATION SEARCH
(find what changes, contrasts, repeats, converges, recontextualizes, or lands as consequence)
    |
    v
CREATIVE OPPORTUNITY RANKING
(choose the strongest grounded relationship)
    |
    v
LATENT MOVIE / DRAMATIC PROGRESSION
(turn the chosen relationship into a sequence of attention changes)
    |
    v
LENS TREATMENT
(primary + secondary pressure; never fact generation)
    |
    v
REALIZATION
(express the approved semantic move)
    |
    v
MOUTH
(felt customer language; no internal cognition prose)
    |
    v
STORY / SEQUENCE
    |
    v
EXPERIENCE COMPOSITION
(story + additive media/context/actions)
```

## Authority rules

Reality is immutable source truth.

Metamorphic relations are derived scaffolding. They may explain why supplied events become interesting together, but they cannot create a new event.

Creative opportunities are ranked hypotheses, not facts.

Movie selection is semantic structure: it discovers progression and payoff from the supplied world.

Lens is pressure, not authorship. It may change tone, emphasis, rhythm, implication, contrast, intimacy, unease, status, or compression. It cannot introduce an actor, action, place, outcome, chronology, or event that is not supported.

Realization converts the chosen semantic move into concrete viewer-facing story cuts.

Mouth is language only. It does not become a second semantic planner.

## Story-first composition law

The story is the primary authored experience.

```text
STORY CAPACITY
      ↓
determined by semantic richness
      ↓
NOT reduced for media
```

Photos, video, geo, timestamps, maps, receipts, links, and similar additions are separate experience material. They may be placed before, between, after, or alongside story cuts.

They do not consume story beats and do not force Cognition to choose a shorter Movie.

A business experience may naturally open with location/context and close with departure/context. A before/after experience may place photos around a meaningful change. A wedding may contain dozens of photos while still receiving as much authored story as the reality earns.

Launch tiers may control media count, storage, processing, AI generation, or render complexity. Those controls must never become hidden creative limits.

The frontend may allow the user to reorder additive elements. Reordering changes presentation only; it does not mutate reality, provenance, or the selected Movie.

## Composable lenses

A lens can be represented as a stack:

```text
HEIST + COMEDY
GAME + FIERCE
NOIR + DEADPAN
ROMANCE + SURREAL
HORROR + DOCUMENTARY
```

The stack combines pressure vectors. It does not combine story templates.

Example:

```text
The operation was flawless.
The bow was not.
```

The facts remain the same.

## Universal media examples

### Service

```text
Subject: Coco
Reality: arrived dirty -> bath -> clean -> blue bow -> pickup
```

Possible treatments include comedy, noir, heist, game, fierce, courtroom, military, documentary, romance, or horror. The service business is the arena; Coco remains the star.

### Housekeeping

```text
Maria
kitchen -> bathroom one -> bathroom two
plus optional time / place / photos
```

The authored story decides how much semantic movement the cleaning reality actually earns. Times and geo may appear as supporting context rather than consuming story beats.

### Restaurant / relationship

```text
closed
lights off
chairs on the ceiling
Alex + Sam together
```

A romance treatment can make the environmental strangeness recede behind the relationship without inventing a waiter, kiss, conversation, or intentional closure.

### Memory

```text
Paul
old records
birthday cards
same song every Sunday
gone
```

Memory should not be forced into a genre. QRE should discover the persistence, ritual, absence, and callback structure already present and make that worth revisiting.

### Moving / real estate / car wash

A moving experience can discover an extraction/mission relationship. A house can become lived history rather than advertising. A car wash can make the customer's vehicle the protagonist of the before/after rather than producing an advertisement for the wash business.

### Dog tag / identity

```text
Coco
apples
small dogs
walks
squirrels
bacon
```

Sparse identity facts can become a playful, distinctive sequence without inventing behavior that was never supplied.

## Standard of quality

The key question is not:

> Does this sound like a story?

It is:

> Would the person who actually lived this want to experience it again in five years?

A great QRE realization preserves truth while increasing felt value through semantic progression, not fabricated plot.
