# QRE Author Artist Device — Current Canonical State

## Purpose

QRE Author is a universal creative device, not a domain-specific story generator. It converts supplied reality into a replayable experience while keeping reality immutable and creative expression unconstrained inside the evidence boundary.

## Canonical flow

```text
SUPPLIED REALITY
    ↓
RealityGraph
    ├── Readout  → factual projection only
    └── semantic search
          ↓
Creative Spine / grounded metamorphic opportunities
          ↓
Universal Cognition
    → competing grounded interpretations
    → candidate Movies
          ↓
Independent Experience Judge
    → chooses whether a Movie has earned visibility
          ↓
ONE Movie
          ↓
Creative Realizer / Mouth
    → turns the selected relationship into felt language
    → QRE binds provenance after realization
          ↓
Independent Realized Film Judge
    → judges the actual visible film, not Movie metadata
          ↓
SequencePlay / Experience
```

## The creative contract

The visible film should create:

```text
FACT
→ RELATIONSHIP
→ CHANGE IN WHAT THE VIEWER NOTICES
→ PAYOFF
```

The final payoff is allowed to be an artistic interpretation. It does not need to repeat the vocabulary of the supplied facts. It must be earned by the concrete relationship immediately before it.

Example target:

```text
stolen apple
fierce Coco
↓
Playful defiance.
```

The system must prefer an earned artistic fragment over an explanation such as `this shows playful defiance`.

## Reality boundary

Reality owns:

- events
- actions
- actors
- places
- chronology
- supplied outcomes
- factual observations

Creative freedom owns:

- compression
- juxtaposition
- omission
- repetition
- contrast
- recontextualization
- callback
- rhythm
- status framing
- implication
- metaphorical or interpretive landing
- form and wording

Creative preference, lens selection, and learned taste may never modify the underlying world model.

## Mouth rules

The Mouth receives a selected grounded relationship and creates customer-facing art.

For multi-event films:

1. Cuts before the final landing must visibly touch supplied reality.
2. The film must contain a visible multi-source bridge for the selected relationship.
3. The final cut may leave source vocabulary and become abstract/interpretive.
4. Internal architecture, planner language, reasoning, scoring, provenance IDs, and selection mechanics never appear to the customer.
5. No fabricated people, actions, objects, places, sensory details, reactions, motives, outcomes, or chronology.
6. There is no fixed beat count and no requirement to render one screen per fact.

The form must be driven by the reality. Different worlds should be capable of producing radically different structures.

## Independent judging

There are two distinct quality gates:

### Movie Judge

Determines whether the latent interpretation is sufficiently grounded, specific, distinctive, consequential, and semantically progressive.

### Realized Film Judge

Determines whether the actual visible scenes deliver what the Movie promised. It checks:

- concrete grounding
- relationship bridge
- progression of attention
- earned landing
- form diversity
- unsupported material
- explanation leakage
- caption-reel collapse

A Movie passing does not guarantee a visible film passes.

## Failure behavior

QRE fails closed. It must reject a weak or fabricated visible film rather than expose cognition scaffolding as a customer-facing fallback.

A rejection is a system-quality signal, not permission to weaken the reality boundary.

## Universalization rule

No domain gets its own author. Pets, people, businesses, places, products, services, events, travel, objects, relationships, return visits, and future threads all use the same cognition and realization architecture.

Domain context is vocabulary/context only. It does not select a different creative brain.

## Artist-device principle

The machine should continuously search for semantic leverage already present in reality. It should not continuously search for a familiar story template.

The desired loop is:

```text
Understand
→ Notice
→ Generate competing grounded readings
→ Attack weak readings
→ Judge
→ Create
→ Judge the actual creation
→ Observe
→ Learn presentation preference
→ Create again
```

Learning changes presentation preference, pacing, novelty appetite, and framing preference. Learning never changes truth.

## Current validation rule

A green structural test is not enough. The final `FINAL FILM` output is the real benchmark.

The standard is whether the person who lived the reality would want to experience the resulting creation again years later.
