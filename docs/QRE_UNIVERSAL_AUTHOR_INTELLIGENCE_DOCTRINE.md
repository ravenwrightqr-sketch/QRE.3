# QRE Universal Author Intelligence Doctrine

STATUS: CANONICAL
DATE: 2026-09-05
BRANCH: build/universal-author-local

## Purpose

QRE is a universal experience compiler. It must not assume that every input is a story, a memory, a receipt, a sequence, a business service, or a genre.

The production Author must behave like an intelligent operator:

**Understand → Notice → Judge → Imagine → Learn**

The system may be expressive, surprising, funny, tender, strange, cinematic, practical, or quiet. The treatment changes. Reality does not.

## Intelligence stack

**RealityGraph** establishes factual authority.

**Readout** is the boring factual projection. It exists so reality can be inspected without creative distortion.

**Cognition** identifies what is actually interesting, searches competing grounded meanings, notices relationships and unusual details, decides whether the material has earned a movie, and asks for missing concrete reality when needed.

**Experience Judge** is independent deterministic adjudication between Cognition and realization. It scores semantic movement, grounding, specificity, distinctiveness, attention, consequence, continuity, information density, originality, replay value, caption risk, genericity, repetition, and truth risk.

**Creative Realizer / Mouth** turns the selected meaning into customer language. It is not allowed to invent facts, explain the planner, or repair semantic decisions.

**Sequence / Experience Composition** turns the realization into the final playable experience. Story length follows semantic value. Media, geo, timestamps, attachments, actions, and other context are additive and do not silently consume story capacity.

## Candidate competition

When several grounded readings exist, Cognition should internally generate 4–6 materially different hypotheses.

The hypotheses should vary by semantic mechanism rather than adjectives: contrast, change, recurrence, consequence, convergence, recontextualization, continuation, or distinctive observation.

Each hypothesis is attacked for:

- unsupported invention;
- psychological fill-in;
- generic meaning;
- caption-reel structure;
- metadata narration;
- genre-template dependence;
- fake escalation;
- weak grounding;
- repetition or replay;
- low information density.

Rejected hypotheses remain internal diagnostics. They are never exposed as customer-facing alternatives.

## Universal rules

1. **Do not cover facts for coverage's sake.** A fact can remain evidence without becoming a visible beat.
2. **Do not force plot.** Sparse reality may produce one excellent observation.
3. **Do not force brevity.** Rich reality may earn a longer sequence.
4. **Do not force genre.** A lens is pressure, not a source of events.
5. **Do not force memory language.** A return visit is a world-state problem, not automatically a memory story.
6. **Do not confuse metadata with meaning.** Time, geo, receipts, photos, and attachments are additive unless the reality makes them semantically important.
7. **Do not infer inner life from routine evidence.** Preference, habit, coincidence, or context does not prove motive or emotional state.
8. **Do not let learning rewrite reality.** Learning changes presentation preference only.
9. **Do not let the arena replace the star.** A venue, service, receipt, place, product category, or business context surrounds the subject unless reality establishes otherwise.
10. **Do not render merely because the UI can render.** When the material has not earned an experience, the system should ask for the highest-value missing concrete detail.

## Quality target

The primary product question is:

> Would the person who actually lived this want to experience it again years from now?

Secondary quality signals include:

- materially different readings of the same reality under different lenses;
- concrete nouns and supplied distinctive details survive realization;
- multi-event semantic bridges outperform event-by-event paraphrase;
- the strongest evidence can be used without becoming exposition;
- return experiences have changed readings rather than adjective-swapped replays;
- the output can be quiet when the reality is quiet.

## Hard boundaries

Reality freedom: **0**.

Creative freedom: **high within evidence**.

Learning freedom: **presentation preference only**.

The intelligence stack must remain one universal Author architecture. No PetBrain, BusinessBrain, MemoryBrain, Cognition2, or domain-specific creative brain may be introduced as a substitute for universal cognition.
