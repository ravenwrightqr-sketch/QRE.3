# QRE Universal Persistence + Author Doctrine

## Product category

QRE is a universal persistence layer for real-world things. The physical identifier is the identity doorway, not the product category.

The same underlying system can persist a dog, person, couple, relationship, memorial, rave, marriage, trip, goal, surfboard, bottle cap, product, service, business, restaurant, car, house, property, place, or event.

## Universal loop

```text
REAL THING
  -> QRE IDENTITY
  -> ACCUMULATED REALITY
  -> MEMORY
  -> COGNITION / MEANING
  -> MOVIE / EXPERIENCE
  -> LENS
  -> MOUTH
  -> EXPERIENCE DISTRIBUTION
  -> RETURN
  -> MORE REALITY
```

## Source reality law

Raw human input is preserved as source material with provenance.

QRE may interpret source material creatively, but creative interpretation never becomes source reality.

A statement describing what a thing generally is, does, likes, or tends to do does not authorize QRE to claim that a physical occurrence happened.

Examples:

- `Coco walks in the park.` = habit/general truth; no walk happened merely because this was supplied.
- `Coco loves apples.` = preference; no apple was eaten.
- `Coco is a small dog.` = attribute; no new event exists.
- `Coco walked in the park for 45 minutes today.` = observed occurrence; the movement, place, duration, and temporal anchor are authorized.
- `We walked Coco in the park.` = observed occurrence.

**Facts can be twisted. Events cannot be invented.**

## Author law

Author does not make a separate story product for each domain. Domain is material, not form.

Author searches the complete supplied world for:

- metamorphic relationships
- contradiction
- recurrence
- status changes
- callbacks
- accumulation
- recontextualization
- expectation shifts
- distinctive detail
- active pressure when genuinely supported

Then it realizes the strongest semantic possibility as an experience.

Sparse reality is valid. One excellent cut is better than a fabricated sequence.

## Lens law

Lens changes feeling, stance, pressure, rhythm, metaphor, emphasis, or status around the discovered reality.

Lens never creates concrete facts, actors, events, places, motives, outcomes, or chronology.

The same reality may therefore be realized as comedy, romance, noir, horror, game, fierce, tender, absurd, quiet, or another supported treatment without changing what actually happened.

## Persistence law

A new reality contribution is added to the existing identity/world rather than replacing the world.

The system should become more specific over time:

```text
DAY 1: traits / preferences / habits
DAY 7: actual occurrence
DAY 30: recurrence / callback
LATER: accumulated continuity
```

The Author may use prior supplied reality to make a later experience feel like the same character/world, but it may only treat supplied history as real memory.

## Distribution law

The experience is the distribution mechanism for QRE.

People encounter a real thing, enter its QRE world, experience something interesting, return later, contribute more reality, and cause the world to become richer.

QRE does not need to distribute a QR-code story generator. It distributes experiences attached to persistent real-world identities.

## Business/data mode

The universal reality layer may also produce a straightforward structured/data-oriented view when that is what a business wants.

That is a presentation choice, not a second Author or second persistence architecture.
